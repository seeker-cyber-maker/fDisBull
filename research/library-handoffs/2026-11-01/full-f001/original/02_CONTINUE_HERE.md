## Evidence and execution rules

Use teleports to make exploration efficient, while preserving spatial-list
membership, actor state and attachments. Label diagnostic preparation explicitly.
Successful placement does not prove walking or legitimate mission reachability.
Bounded out-of-context execution is appropriate when a routine's supplied state,
expected effects, exits, fault/timeout limits and cleanup are recorded. Then
backtrack to caller requirements instead of fabricating unexplained success.

Native selector/object mappings are essential. Drivers can unload/reload at new
addresses. MAIN objects need object-specific runtime bases, not one global delta.
Timer callbacks require SS-aware stack interpretation. Do not treat EAX as a
universal success flag or stopped/released state as natural completion.
