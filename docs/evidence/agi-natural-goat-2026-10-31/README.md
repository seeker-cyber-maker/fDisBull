# Ordinary KQ1 goat encounter

`receipt.json` binds the source, backend and implementation to a fresh private
ordinary-input replay: acquire the carrot, open the gate, approach/lure the goat
and lead it into room 9. The score changes from 2 to 7 while the carrot remains
carried. A naturally entered logic-103 invocation follows 18 source offsets and
returns through its original caller with no declared projection differences.

The three PNGs are unedited stored native frames. They support inspection of
the visible goat, original lure dialogue and following outside the pen. RAM
drawn flags are retained separately and do not define screenshot visibility.

The receipt also records original VIEW mirror-cache writes, trace hashes,
settled image equivalence and rejection of a partial transformation. Source
and runtime VIEW hashes remain distinct. Original VIEW payloads and traces are
retained privately, alongside the game copies, native states and databases.

A separate completed replay supplied the portable Z export, consumed by the
installed 0.27.0 player and content service in separate processes. Its checkpoint
test is in-memory only. Original story text and the generated scene stay private.

See [the native/export contract](../../AGI_NATURAL_GOAT.md) for reproduction,
observed coverage, supplied-evidence limits and the unavailable Linux CI gate.
