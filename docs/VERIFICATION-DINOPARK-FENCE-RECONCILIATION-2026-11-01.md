## Failure and corrected boundary

The preceding wood-fence trial accepted seven inputs, ending at opening the map.
Its 60-second client allowance expired and the worker/native processes exited.
No fence commit certificate was captured. That original effect remains unknown;
it was not replayed, restored or reconnected.

The legacy purchase requested at least 71,300 guest milliseconds across twelve
clicks, plus readiness polls, without a shared Z action deadline. A private
observer wrapper substituted 120 seconds for the incoming run allowance. The
composed operation could outlive the client's 60-second request. Production
transport failure closes the connection and cleans up the owned process group,
losing the guest needed for reconciliation. Worker -9 and native absence do not
independently establish a native crash, rollback or signal sender.

The new handler preserves all twelve click points and their order. It prepares
the pointer, checks fresh source/release conditions, queues each press once and
polls its phase. Z binds a shared 40-second native deadline before observation.
Preparation must fit both host and cumulative guest allowances before movement.
Each phase retains a 16,000-millisecond requested-guest cap across continuations.
Uncertain delivery/execution does not authorize replay.

`operation_budget.request_deadline` intersects the incoming allowance with the
outer native deadline. The RPC decorator uses it. Corrected private observer
composition also calculates it once before timing/native reads, propagates it
to callbacks and retains it across owned stops. Measured guest progress and
unexpected/unowned stop outcomes are preserved; hooks do not renew the budget.

guest milliseconds at our own passive `asset_trace` actor-entry breakpoint.
Its native outcome was `hook`, `timed_out=false`, with about 39.6 seconds

with unknown navigation effects and no execution/input/restore; the original

The observer wrapper correctly honors `stop_on_hook=True`; both scene wait paths
also requested that policy and treated every hook as terminal. The registration
policy in candidate F3 (`b7bc256168226f8f23918dcae09335bb62364578`) resolves this
composition mismatch. Exact observer breakpoint IDs are explicitly bound to the
run, source version and runtime epoch. Only one matching registered native hook
can be consumed. Unknown/mixed/unregistered stops and intentional debugger stops
still stop. Raw `run(stop_on_hook=True)` semantics are unchanged.

Each consumed stop is stored in a linked project observation chain before
continuing. Replies include at most sixteen recent metadata references and a
total count. Native progress is measured, the original guest/host/poll limits
remain in force, and no action input is repeated. Failed evidence persistence
cannot authorize another run. See [scene framework](SCENE_FRAMEWORK.md).
